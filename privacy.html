Privacy

Last updated 20 November 2026

This page explains what happens to information when you use diyhomes. The guides are here to read. Nothing about using them asks you to sign in.

What this site collects.

These pages do not ask for an account and do not collect form submissions. Opening a page asks the host to send that page and its images to your browser, which may leave ordinary access logs.

When you write to us.

If you send an email using an address on this site, we receive the address and the message. We use it to reply to you, and for nothing else.

Cookies and measurement.

These pages set no advertising cookies and run no third-party tracking. Your browser may keep a copy of a page in its normal cache.

Photographs and links.

Some photographs come from Wikimedia Commons and are credited on the home page. Links to other sites lead to their own privacy practices.

Sharing.

We do not sell personal information. We do not pass the contents of a message to advertisers.

Keeping and deleting.

We keep correspondence only as long as we need it to answer you. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Changes.

If these practices change, this page will change with them, and the date at the top will be updated.

Contact.

Questions about this page can go to the diyhomes site owner.
